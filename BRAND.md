# Fedintel — Brand Identity & Manifesto

## Name

**Fedintel** — Federal Opportunity Intelligence. Blunt, memorable, says what
it does. As we add state/local coverage the parent framing becomes simply
"Fedintel — Opportunity Intelligence," and the name still holds: *intel* is
the product, *fed* is the origin story.

## Positioning

**The opportunity radar for people who build software.**
Not a database. Not a portal. A judgment engine that reads everything so a
small software firm reads only what matters.

## Voice

Plain, confident, operator-to-operator. We write like a sharp capture
manager texting a founder: "This one's real. Sources Sought, Army, inventory
modernization, small-biz set-aside, due the 29th. Move."
Never breathless, never "revolutionize," never buzzword salad.

## Visual identity

- **Two-world palette:** deep navy/near-black (`#0B1220`) for the working
  dashboard — this is night-ops, signal-on-dark; clean white with slate text
  for email — morning briefing on paper. The mockups already live this split;
  keep it deliberate.
- **Accent blue** `#4F8FF7` for the "intel" mark and interactive elements;
  **signal green** `#177A4C` reserved exclusively for match scores ≥80 —
  green means *act*.
- **The match badge is the logo in miniature:** a rounded square holding a
  number. Wherever a score appears, the brand appears.
- Type: a grotesque sans (Inter/Söhne class) everywhere; scores set heavy
  (800), metadata quiet. No serifs, no ornament — this is an instrument.

## Manifesto

**We read the Federal Register so you can write software.**

Somewhere in today's flood of notices is a government team drowning in a
spreadsheet that should be an application, a warehouse tracked on paper, a
legacy system whose maintainers have retired. That notice is an invitation —
and it is almost impossible to find.

We believe the government deserves modern software, and that the firms best
equipped to build it are small, fast, and chronically too busy building to
hunt. So we hunt.

We are great at this because we refuse the easy version. We don't dump
keyword matches in your inbox and call it intelligence. We score like a
builder scores: is the real work software? Can a small firm win it? Is it
early enough to shape? We track a pursuit from Sources Sought to award like
it's our own — because before it was your radar, it was ours, and we used it
to win our own work.

We will be the best at this because we compound where others coast: every
win and loss tunes the scoring, every year of lineage history deepens a
dataset no one can backfill, every new adapter widens coverage from federal
to fifty states to every city with a portal. Reliability is our brand —
the digest arrives at 6:30 every morning, even when the fancy parts fail.

And we will build a great company doing it the same way we tell our users to
build: boring infrastructure, sharp judgment, small team, high trust,
customers who win contracts and tell their friends. No venture theater. No
growth hacks. Just an instrument that earns its place in your morning —
one true signal at a time.

**Modern software for the government starts with finding the door.
We find doors.**
